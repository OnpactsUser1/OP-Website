Everything in OnPacts revolves around the week. It's long enough to fit real life, with its busy days and quiet ones, and short enough that a fresh start is never far away. Once you understand how a week flows, the rest of the app makes a lot of sense.

Here's the full cycle, step by step.

## The week runs Monday to Sunday

Every OnPacts week starts on Monday and ends on Sunday, based on your phone's time zone. There's no rolling seven-day window to keep track of. Monday is always day one.

While a week is running, it's **locked**. You can't change the number of days or your time slot halfway through. That's deliberate: a commitment that can be edited whenever it gets inconvenient isn't much of a commitment.

If you need flexibility during the week, that's what leaves are for, and we'll come back to them in a moment.

## Planning happens before the week

You plan your upcoming week using **Manage Upcoming Week**. You decide two things:

1. **How many days you'll work out.** Pick a number you're confident about, not the most heroic number you can think of.
2. **Your 3-hour time slot.** This is the window each day when you'll do your sessions, such as 6 AM to 9 AM before work, or 6 PM to 9 PM after it.

You can plan as early as you like and change your mind as often as you like, right up until the deadline.

## The Sunday 11:55 PM lock

The planning window closes at **Sunday 11:55 PM**. Until then, for the upcoming week you can:

- set or change your plan,
- adjust your time slot,
- or choose to take a break.

At 11:55 PM on Sunday, the upcoming week locks, and a few minutes later it begins. That small gap gives everything time to settle before Monday starts.

A good habit is to treat Sunday evening as a two-minute check-in. Look at the week ahead, notice anything unusual such as travel, late meetings or a family event, and set a plan that fits it.

## Forgot to plan? Auto Week Planner has you covered

Life gets busy, and sometimes Sunday night slips by. If you didn't plan by the deadline, **Auto Week Planner** steps in:

- If you've saved a **default plan**, it's applied to the new week automatically.
- If you haven't, the week becomes an **automatic break**.

Saving a default plan is one of the most useful things you can do in your first week. It means a forgotten Sunday never costs you a week of progress.

## Breaks: no gain, no loss

A break week has no commitment at all. You can still be active, but there's nothing to keep and nothing to miss. Break weeks have **no gain and no loss**, so take one when you genuinely need a week off, without guilt.

## During the week: perform in your slot

On each committed day, you start an activity, either steps or calories, and your phone's sensors track and verify it live. When you're done, you check out.

Two rules are worth remembering:

- **A completed activity counts toward your weekly goal**, wherever in the day you check out.
- **Only days checked out inside your time slot earn rewards.** Checking out outside the slot still counts toward the goal, but isn't rewarded.

Tracking keeps going with your screen off, so you can pocket your phone and get on with it.

## Leaves: the flexibility inside the lock

You get **2 leaves per month**. A leave can be used during an active week for a committed day whose goal is still open. It counts toward your committed days, so your week stays intact, but a leave day doesn't earn a reward. Leaves reset on the 1st and don't roll over.

## How the week settles

When Sunday ends, the week is done and OnPacts works out the result:

- If **every committed day** was fulfilled by a completed activity or a leave, the week earns a reward for the days you checked out inside your slot.
- If a committed day was missed, that week earns no reward. **Nothing is ever deducted.**

Rewards are credited after the week settles on Monday. In Weekly History, the week shows as **Pending** on Monday and Tuesday, and the amount appears from Wednesday.

## Joined mid-week?

If you sign up partway through a week, that week is a **Demo week** that runs through Sunday. There's no commitment, and completed days are still rewarded. It's a relaxed way to learn the rhythm before your first full week begins.

## The whole cycle in one line

Plan before Sunday 11:55 PM, perform inside your slot on the days you chose, and let the week settle. Then do it again, one week at a time.
