This policy explains how **OnPacts Limited** ("OnPacts", "we", "us") handles personal information when you use the OnPacts app (on Android, and on iOS once released) and this website. We collect what we need to run the service: measuring your activity, verifying it fairly, paying rewards and supporting you.

## What we collect and why

### Account data

Your name, email address, Google sign-in identity (if you sign in with Google), profile details you add, and your time zone. We use these to create and secure your account, show your profile, and run your weeks in your local time.

### Activity data

Steps, calories, workout start and end times, live scores, sensor confidence scores and checkout times. We use these to measure your activities, check them against your plan and time slot, calculate rewards, and build your Weekly History, discipline score, streaks and insights.

To measure and verify activities, the app uses your phone's **sensors** (step counter and accelerometer) and **location** (GPS, during a workout only). [CONFIRM: whether GPS coordinates are stored on our servers, or only used on the phone to compute distance and the sensor confidence score]

### Wallet and withdrawals

Your wallet balance and reward history, and the **bank or PayPal details** you enter to receive withdrawals (for example bank name, account number, SWIFT, IBAN or IFSC code, or PayPal email). We use these only to pay you and keep payment records. [CONFIRM: payout processor that receives these details]

### Support messages

Messages and attachments you send us through the app, this website or email, so we can help you and keep a record of the conversation.

### Device and diagnostics

- **Crash reports** through Firebase Crashlytics, to find and fix bugs.
- **App analytics** through Google Analytics for Firebase, to understand which features are used.
- **Push notification tokens** through Firebase Cloud Messaging, to send workout progress and reminders.
- **Device integrity checks**, such as detecting rooted or jailbroken devices and checking that your clock and time zone are set automatically, to keep rewards fair.

## Ads

The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, and, if you allow it, to personalise them.

Your choices:

- On most Android phones, open **Settings, then Google, then Ads** (or **Settings, then Privacy, then Ads**) to delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads.
- On iPhone, the app asks your permission before using your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in **Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking**. [CONFIRM: whether the iOS app requests App Tracking Transparency permission]
- Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app. [CONFIRM: consent tool used, e.g. Google User Messaging Platform]
- Learn more in [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Storage and processors

- **Account and activity data** are stored with our backend provider, **Supabase**.
- **Google and Firebase services** (Google Sign-In, Crashlytics, Analytics for Firebase, Cloud Messaging and AdMob) process data as described above.
- Your **profile photo is stored only on your phone**. We don't upload it.
- Payout details are shared with our payment provider only to make withdrawals. [CONFIRM: payout processor]

We don't sell your personal information.

## Permissions explained

- **Physical activity (Motion & Fitness on iPhone):** lets the app read your step counter and motion sensors to measure workouts.
- **Location (while using the app):** used during workouts only, to measure movement and support the sensor confidence score.
- **Notifications:** show workout progress while you exercise, and reminders about your plan and time slot.
- **Foreground service (Android):** keeps a workout running, with a visible notification, when your screen is off.
