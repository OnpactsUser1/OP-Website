You can delete your OnPacts account and associated data at any time. This page applies to the **OnPacts : Commit to Fitness** app by OnPacts Limited.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open OnPacts and go to **Edit Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Tell us why you're leaving, in **50 to 200 words**.
4. **Confirm twice**.

Deletion is **irreversible**. Your plans, history, streaks and wallet balance can't be restored, so withdraw any eligible balance first.

## Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email **support@onpacts.com** from the address on your account with the subject **"Account & Data Deletion"**. We may ask you to verify that the account is yours before deleting it. [CONFIRM: response time for email deletion requests, e.g. within 30 days]

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymise your account data (name, email, sign-in identity and profile details), your activity data (steps, calories, workout times, scores and checkouts), your plans, leaves, history and community connections, and your push notification tokens. Your profile photo is stored only on your phone, so uninstalling the app removes it. [CONFIRM: complete list and deletion timeframe, e.g. within 30 days]

## What may be kept, and for how long

We may keep a limited amount of information where the law requires it or where it's needed for legitimate purposes:

- **Payment and withdrawal records** for tax, accounting and anti-fraud obligations. [CONFIRM: retention period]
- **Support conversations** related to an open dispute or legal claim. [CONFIRM: retention period]
- **Fraud and abuse records**, such as device integrity flags, to prevent repeated abuse. [CONFIRM: retention period]
- **Backups** that are overwritten on a regular cycle. [CONFIRM: backup retention period]
- **Crash and analytics data** held by Google/Firebase under their own retention settings. [CONFIRM: Firebase data retention settings]
