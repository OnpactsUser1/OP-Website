## The app is free

OnPacts has **no paid plans, subscriptions or in-app purchases** today, so there is nothing to charge and nothing to refund. If we add paid features in the future, this page will describe their pricing and refund terms before they launch.

## Rewards

You earn rewards by keeping your weekly commitments. See section 8, Rewards, of our [Terms and conditions](/terms-and-conditions/#8-rewards) for the full rules. In short:

- Rewards are credited after a committed week settles, and only if every committed day was fulfilled by a completed activity or a leave.
- Only days checked out inside your time slot earn rewards. Leave days earn nothing.
- Rewards vary from week to week. They are **not wages, prizes or gambling winnings**.
- **Nothing is ever deducted** from your wallet for missed weeks.

## Withdrawals

- Withdraw from **$5 minimum to $50 maximum** per request, up to your wallet balance.
- Go to Account settings, then **Withdraw Rewards**.
- Choose a **bank transfer** (bank name, account number, and SWIFT, IBAN or IFSC code) or **PayPal**.
- A withdrawal shows as **Pending** until it is paid or fails.

## Failed or incorrect payouts

A payout can fail if account details are incorrect, the account can't receive the transfer, or the provider rejects it. When a payout fails, its status changes to failed and the amount is returned to your wallet so you can correct your details and try again. [CONFIRM: whether failed amounts return to the wallet automatically]

If a payout looks wrong, for example the amount is incorrect or it shows as paid but hasn't arrived, email **Payments@onpacts.com** with the **withdrawal date and amount**, and the email address on your account. We'll investigate and reply.

## Fraud and abuse

To keep OnPacts fair, rewards that come from manipulated activity, rooted or jailbroken devices, changed clocks or time zones, or falsified data may be withheld or reversed, and the account may be suspended.

## Contact

Payments and withdrawals: **Payments@onpacts.com**. Everything else: **support@onpacts.com**.
