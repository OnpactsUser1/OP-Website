## 1. What OnPacts is

OnPacts is a fitness discipline app. You commit to a weekly fitness plan, perform it, and are rewarded only for what you actually did.

- **Not gambling.** No luck is involved, you never stake money, and nothing is ever deducted from your wallet. Rewards follow the commitments you keep.

## 3. Weeks and planning

- Every OnPacts week runs **Monday to Sunday**.
- Before a week begins, you choose how many days you'll work out and a **3-hour time slot** for those sessions.
- Once a week starts, it is locked. You plan the next one using **Manage Upcoming Week**.
- You can plan, change your plan, or take a break for the upcoming week until **Sunday 11:55 PM**. After that, the upcoming week is locked.
- If you didn't plan by the deadline, **Auto Week Planner** applies your saved default plan, or an automatic break if you don't have one.

All times are based on your phone's time zone.

## 4. Activities

- An activity is either a **steps** session (walking or running) or a **calories** session (training). Your phone's sensors track and verify it, and the app saves it.
- A completed activity counts toward your weekly goal. To earn a reward for that day, you must **check out inside your time slot**. Checking out outside the slot still counts toward the goal, but isn't rewarded.
- Tracking continues with the screen off, using a foreground service and a visible notification.
- If the app is interrupted, come back within **5 minutes** to continue. Otherwise the activity is checked out automatically and sent to us when your phone is next online.
- Only sensor-verified activity counts. We may reject activity that looks manipulated, simulated or inconsistent with sensor data.

## 5. Your phone

- **Automatic date, time and time zone** must be switched on. Activities recorded with a manually changed clock or mismatched time zone may be blocked or not counted.
- **Rooted or jailbroken phones are not supported.** The app may refuse to run on rooted, jailbroken or otherwise modified devices to keep rewards fair.
- The app needs the permissions described in our [Privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) to measure and verify activity.

## 6. Commitments, leaves and breaks

- Each committed day must be fulfilled by a completed activity or a leave.
- You get **2 leaves per month**. A leave can be used only during an active week, while that day's goal is still open.
- Each leave needs a short reason of **100 to 300 words**.
- A leave counts toward your committed days, but **leave days earn no reward**.
- Leaves reset on the **1st of each month**. Unused leaves don't roll over.
- A **break week** has no commitment: no gain and no loss.

## 7. Demo week

If you join in the middle of a week, that week is a **Demo week** that runs through Sunday. There's no commitment during a Demo week, and completed days are still rewarded. Your first full week starts the following Monday.

## 8. Rewards

- Reward amounts vary from week to week. They are not wages, prizes or winnings, and they are not guaranteed.
- On a committed week, rewards are credited to your in-app wallet **after the week settles on Monday**, and only if **every committed day** was fulfilled by a completed activity or a leave.
- Only days checked out inside your time slot earn rewards. Leave days earn nothing.
- In Weekly History, a settled week shows **"Pending" on Monday and Tuesday**, and the reward amount **from Wednesday**.
- If you miss a committed day, that week earns no reward. **Nothing is ever deducted** from your wallet.
- We may withhold or reverse rewards that came from manipulated activity, rooted or jailbroken devices, falsified data or any breach of these terms.
